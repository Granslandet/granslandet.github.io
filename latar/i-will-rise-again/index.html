 "I will rise again,"  She said.  "I have been down at the bottom,  Where the deepest sorrows rest." No one ever told me life would be easy, But no one said how hard the grief could be. How it settles in the silence, How it takes its time with me.  "I will rise again,"  She said.  "I have been down at the bottom,  Where the deepest sorrows rest." I carry all my memories with me, As I wander through the town. Snow is lying thick upon the pavement, And its silver settles softly in my hair.  "I will rise again,"  She said.  "I have been down at the bottom,  Where the deepest sorrows rest." Sometimes I just need to lie here for a while, Watch the faces of my sorrow spinning round, All around me. But you don't have to worry, You don't have to be afraid. Because...  "I will rise again,"  She said.  "I have been down at the bottom,  Where the deepest sorrows rest." Tomorrow I will smile again, At everything we used to do. And when I look back on those days, I'll remember the friendship that we knew.  "I will rise again,"  She said.  "I have been down at the bottom,  Where the deepest sorrows rest."
